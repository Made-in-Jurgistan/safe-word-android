# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience.
Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and attentive to the
impact of their words and actions.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
unwelcome sexual attention, publishing private information, and other conduct
that would reasonably make a participant feel unsafe or unable to contribute.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to `madeinjurgistan@gmail.com`. Reports
will be reviewed and handled fairly and confidentially where possible. The
maintainer may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation
when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies in project spaces and whenever an individual represents the
project publicly.
